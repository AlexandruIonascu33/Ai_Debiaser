Informed Consent and Study Overview

Purpose: This study examines how people evaluate employees whose leadership potential is under consideration in an organizational setting.

Your role: Please act as a workplace rater or evaluator. For each profile, imagine that you are assessing a real employee and consider whether the available information suggests they could effectively guide and support other people. Make your judgments as carefully as you would in that situation.

What you will do
  • You will evaluate four candidate profiles. For each profile, assess the person's leadership potential, complete the rating scales, and set the bonus-allocation slider based on the information initially shown.
  • Every rating item must be answered, and you must move the bonus slider before submitting. You may move it back to $0 if that is your decision.
  • Once you submit the first evaluation for a candidate, you cannot return to or change it.
  • You will then see additional performance information. Before completing the second and final evaluation, you must write a brief justification for your initial judgments.
  • In the final evaluation, you may keep your original ratings or revise them after considering the new information and your justification.
  • Please answer independently, use only the information available in the study, and take the instruction-check items seriously.

There are no correct ratings. We are interested in your own considered judgments and the reasoning you provide.

Participation is voluntary and you may stop at any time. Your responses will be used for research purposes.
